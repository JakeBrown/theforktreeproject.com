We are excited to announce a collaboration between The Forktree Project and Prince’s Trust Australia. With its commitment to the development of young people and helping communities create a sustainable future, Prince’s Trust Australia is the ideal charitable partner for The Forktree Project.

We greatly look forward to working with Prince’s Trust Australia to develop education programs at Forktree for young people and the broader community about the importance of environmental stewardship, both through the provision of formal educational content as well as the modeling of sustainable practices. 

For more information on this collaboration, [click here](<https://www.princes-trust.org.au/sustainable-communities/climate-action-through-education-forktree/>).
