The Forktree Project couldn’t happen without a lot of hard graft, and the generosity of volunteers. We are in the thick of planting season now, and we are hugely grateful to the volunteers, friends old and new, who have visited Forktree over the last few weekends, enabling us to get the saplings in the ground quickly and efficiently. 

Good for the trees, good for the environment, but also good for the soul - there’s no doubt that helping out is beneficial in so many ways. Maybe volunteering is your secret power too - if you fancy helping out at Forktree, please get in touch. We’d love to hear from you!
