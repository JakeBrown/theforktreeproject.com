As we get closer to the beginning of planting season 2024, the saplings in the Forktree Project nursery are looking great! 

All local provenance, all native species endemic to the region, all chosen to boost biodiversity locally. Thank you, nursery managers Fleur and Ida - your charges look super healthy! 

Landowners and reveg project managers: please get in touch if you are looking for tube stock for this planting season - we would love to help you out and work with you. Contact via our website: www.the forktree project.com.
