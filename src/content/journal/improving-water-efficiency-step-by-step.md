We’re excited to be taking the next step in developing water efficiency at Forktree with these soil moisture probes and tank level meters which have now been installed and connected to the LoRaWAN by the ever-cheerful Efrim from Fleetspace.

This is all part of of our drive to maximise water efficiency via automation at Forktree. A big thanks to SA Water and Fleetspace for helping us with this.
