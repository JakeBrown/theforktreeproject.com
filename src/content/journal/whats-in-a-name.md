Finding the right name for something can be tricky, but with the Forktree Project it was straightforward.

The name was initially chosen because the property is bordered at one end by Forktree Road, a high road notable for its spectacular coastal views. 

However, the name seemed to work on a symbolic level too - humankind finds itself at a fork in the road, and each of us needs to choose the route ahead wisely and do our bit, whether large or small, to save the planet from the ravages of climate change.

![](/images/journal/whats-in-a-name-1.jpg)

*The property itself seems to approve of the name The Forktree Project…*
