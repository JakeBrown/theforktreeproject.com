What an honour to have welcomed Operation Flinders and the 20 impressive young people on their Next Step Program to The Forktree Project last week for a day of volunteering (including nursery work, mulching and landscaping), and two nights of camping. 

We were super excited to see our newly established camping areas put to good use - and we cannot think of a more worthy group to be our first Forktree campers!

Thank you, Operation Flinders - we feel proud to have been part of the amazing work you do, and we look forward to welcoming you to The Forktree Project again soon.
