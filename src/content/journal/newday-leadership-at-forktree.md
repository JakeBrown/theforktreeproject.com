What a fantastic day of stimulating content at The Forktree Project yesterday with leaders from the Adelaide Hills, Fleurieu and Kangaroo Island.  
  
The Forktree Project was delighted to host Session 2 of the Newday Inspired Leadership 2023, a six-month regional leadership program designed and delivered by Newday Leadership in partnership with Regional Development Australia Adelaide Hills, Fleurieu and Kangaroo Island (RDA_AHFKI).

In keeping with the name of Newday’s leadership program, it was inspiring to share the vision of The Forktree Project with this group of regional leaders, all focused on sustainability, regeneration, community and connection. We look forward to hosting other groups for workshops and team-building at The Forktree Project in the future; please feel free to contact us to enquire.  
  
Thank you to Katrina Webb and Matthew Wright-Simon from Newday for facilitating a superb day of learnings from the SAHMRI Be Well Program. Regenerative leadership + purpose = enhanced wellbeing - right up our alley here at Forktree.
