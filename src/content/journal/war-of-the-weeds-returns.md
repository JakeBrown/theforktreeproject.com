On any given day the battle with a particular batch of weeds might seem to have been won, but the war is far from over. Spring rain is as good for weeds as it is for saplings. Many thanks to the volunteers who have helped with this huge task - your input is invaluable. 

If you feel you’ve missed out and fancy getting stuck in to some serious weeding, it’s not too late! Check out the Forktree Project’s Facebook page for details of the next volunteering opportunity at Forktree. We’d love to see you there.
