‘Tis the season… for native grass seed harvesting at The Forktree Project. 

Much excitement here as we collect seed from an area of _rytidosperma_ (wallaby grass) planted in 2022 by numerous volunteers and school groups. 

The more native grasses we plant, the better for the wider ecosystem and the safer for us - native grasses like _rytidosperma_ have a fuel load of 2-5 tonnes per hectare compared to 10-25 tonnes of fuel load for introduced grasses. 

Which would you rather have on your property…??
