Big thanks to Corey Jackson, NRM Coastal Conservation Officer for the Southern Fleurieu Peninsula, for donating 1,000 pre-loved corflutes to the cause. Recycle and reuse is what it’s all about at Forktree.

Every dollar saved by reusing items is another dollar to spend on trees. Every plastic corflute reused is one less in landfill or the recycling depot.

It’s a win-win.
