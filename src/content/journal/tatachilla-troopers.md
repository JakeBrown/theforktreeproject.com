Last Tuesday saw an amazing volunteering effort by Year 9 students from Tatachilla Lutheran College at The Forktree Project. Equipped with gloves, safety goggles and a healthy dose of good cheer, the 94 students tirelessly sorted through building waste, collected rubble for use in gabion walls, cleaned and stacked bricks, and weeded and landscaped around newly planted saplings - Service Learning at its very best and a great example of what can be achieved by teamwork.

Many thanks to Tatachilla Lutheran College staff and students for their enthusiasm for the project and for their generous support - it is hugely appreciated.
