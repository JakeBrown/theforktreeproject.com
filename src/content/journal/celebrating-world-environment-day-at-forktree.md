What better way to celebrate World Environment Day than with our first sighting of an echidna meandering on the hillside amongst the saplings!

This beautiful, shy creature was spotted wandering amongst the melaleuca saplings that we planted last year around our grand old 300-year-old melaleuca, hitherto a lone sentinel in the middle of the Forktree property. 

The byline of World Environment Day 2021 is **“REIMAGINE. RECREATE. RESTORE.”** We are proud to be putting these words into action at The Forktree Project. 

If we all do our bit, wherever and however we can, we can make a difference.
