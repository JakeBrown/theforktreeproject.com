*Hope for the future: young hands planting young trees with care.* 

We were delighted to welcome Yr 6/7 students from Annesley Junior School to The Forktree Project on Monday. The students did an amazing job, applying themselves to tree planting and brick sorting with equal gusto. 

One of the aims of The Forktree Project is to inspire young people to be responsible custodians of the planet both now and in the future. By involving young people in the project we hope to help them understand that we can all have a role, large or small, in combatting climate change.
