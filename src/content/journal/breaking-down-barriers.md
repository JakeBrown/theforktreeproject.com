In a project such as Forktree, a stock-take of tasks achieved is a great motivator every now and then.

Today’s stock-take is of fences and barbed wire. Over the last 3 years we have removed hundreds of metres of old stock fence posts and barbed wire to restore the natural lay of the land.

Removing each post is a full-on work out in itself, as many of our volunteers will attest. A huge thank you to them all.
