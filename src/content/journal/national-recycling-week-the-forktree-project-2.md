More pallet repurposing at The Forktree Project - this time turning old pallets into potting tables for the nursery. 

Thank you to volunteers Ralph and Jake for their creative carpentry skills which they put to good use making these tables, and to the many school students from numerous schools who painted the tables to weatherproof them. 

Team repurposing, all part of the circular economy. Pallet-tastic.
