We are really excited to have the support of Tymaline Building Services at The Forktree Project. TBS serves the strata and insurance sectors and is helping us build a more sustainable future by generously donating to the development of Forktree’s onsite sustainability education centre. 

Strata is an industry that services the apartment buildings sector and has a huge role to play in sustainability given that 75% of people will live in cities by 2050 and many of those people will live in apartment buildings. 

Thank you, TBS, for your generous support of The Forktree Project.
