As the days begin to cool and we inch our way towards winter and the planting season, the Forktree volunteer days are picking up pace. The last two Sundays have seen a fantastic effort by merry bands of volunteers, who have done sterling work battling weeds in preparation for the planting that will begin again soon.

Many thanks as ever to all those who volunteer at Forktree. We couldn’t do it without you, and it is always a pleasure to welcome you to the project. 

To anyone who would like to get involved, please contact us via the “Join Us” page on this website. We’d love to hear from you.
