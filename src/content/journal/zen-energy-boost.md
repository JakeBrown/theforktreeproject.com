What an amazing day we had yesterday at The Forktree Project with the team from Zen Energy and Salesforce! It has been a case study in what can be achieved when a group of willing individuals pull together to use their varied skillsets and combined energy and enthusiasm for the common good.

With around 40 pairs of hands, we made light work of planting hundreds of native trees and shrubs, digging up countless invasive weeds, pulling out redundant fence posts and digging a trench for a stormwater pipe. 

All in the name of restoring habitat and helping the planet heal - and in so doing having a great time!

A huge thank you to all the volunteers from Zen and Salesforce - you are legends, one and all.
