Taking a breather from cleaning bricks left on the property by the former owner. Every brick we clean is one less that ends up in landfill. The half bricks and rubble we’re crushing to make aggregate as a base for our shed, tracks and rainwater tanks.

Australia generates 20 million tonnes of building waste each year. Almost half ends up in landfill. Let’s do what we can to change this.
