Fantastic to see new growth coming from one of our transplanted yaccas (grass trees). 

_Xanthorrhoea semiplana_ are notoriously difficult to transplant, and we had to move this one and 13 other 100-year-old specimens 8 months ago to stop them being destroyed as part of a council road widening project.

Amazingly, most are alive and seem to be recovering. The resilience of nature is as incredible as the original crazy decision to bulldoze them.
