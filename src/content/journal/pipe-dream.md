Wonderful to have the support of Andrew Fairney and Seeding Natives for our work in setting up the Forktree rare seed orchard, through their generous donation of one and a half kilometres of dripper line to be installed for this year’s plantings in the orchard. 

The challenge now is to lay the line out over the beds so that it serves the needs of the various rare species we will be growing in this space: different species require different spacings according to their specific needs.

Two hundred metres installed so far; only one thousand three hundred to go….

Thank you, Andrew Fairney, for your support and expertise. We are hugely grateful.
