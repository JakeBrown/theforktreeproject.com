As if the rainbow photograph we posted a few months ago wasn’t spectacular enough, today we were rewarded after a wild and woolly day of planting and weed clearance by an even more stunning aerial masterpiece. Marking out the gate just like the previous rainbow, this wrap-around arc seemed to be enveloping The Forktree Project approvingly in a wide embrace.

There’s always a danger of waxing too lyrical about these things, but it’s hard not to when faced with such beauty - surely a sign that we are doing something good here!
