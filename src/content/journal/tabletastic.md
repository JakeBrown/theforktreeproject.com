What an amazingly productive few days at the Forktree Project! As if by magic, the pile of donated, pre-used pallets has been transformed into 50 potting tables for the nursery, with struts and legs fashioned from timber lengths salvaged from the building waste left on site. Many thanks to Tim and Jake for building the tables at a lightning pace.

Next step: painting the tables to weatherproof them before they are put into position on the newly finished nursery pad and reticulation infrastructure is set up.
