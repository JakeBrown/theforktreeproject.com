To mark National Recycling Week we are taking a look at all things circular economy at The Forktree Project, and are celebrating not just recycling but repurposing. The circular economy is a huge part of the Forktree Project Story, so the next few posts will take a look at a few of our favourite repurposing projects to date.

First up: We have got a thing about turning pallets that are no longer needed into something useful… like these windbreak fences. 

The circular economy: giving objects a new life to keep them out of landfill and at the same time avoiding the need for new materials to be cut down or mined. What’s not to like? 

Thank you to Power Mowers for donating these pallets.
