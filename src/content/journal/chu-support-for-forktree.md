We are delighted that CHU, Australia’s leading strata insurance specialist, has signed a three-year agreement with the Forktree Project. CHU has agreed to offset its carbon footprint by planting thousands of trees at Forktree. CHU CEO, Bobby Lehane, urges other businesses to follow suit. Many thanks to Bobby and all at CHU.
