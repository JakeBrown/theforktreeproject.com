The end of another day of planting. It’s a great feeling to look down the gully and see the late afternoon sun illuminating the corflutes like candles to the future.

Shielded by these corflutes are a variety of native shrubs, grasses and trees that should thrive in a gully like this. We hope that in time these saplings will create a rich and varied habitat for native birds and other fauna, making this gully even more beautiful.
