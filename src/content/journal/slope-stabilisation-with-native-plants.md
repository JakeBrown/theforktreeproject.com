Today saw Forktree stalwarts Ralph and Jake harvesting Plains Sedge (_Carex bichenoviana_) from Paul Green’s Myponga property for planting at The Forktree Project. 

Paul recommended the Carex as an ideal native plant to stabilise the slope outside our native seed nursery. We are grateful as ever for his advice and support.

**Images:**

_Top left:_ harvesting the plants from Paul’s property. Thank you, Paul!

_Top right:_ getting the plants in the ground to stabilise the slope below the seed nursery

 _Bottom left_ : rocks salvaged on site add extra stabilisation.

_Bottom right:_ all done and ready to grow.
