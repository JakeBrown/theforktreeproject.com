42 tonnes and 6 months of painstaking work later and the nursery retaining wall is complete! (90% made of old building rubble topped with local quarry rock.) 

Very happy with it. Circular economy, recycling and zero waste principles all round.

Thank you to everyone who has helped with this task, from Year 9 students from Tatachilla Lutheran College to Forktree volunteers extraordinaire Jake and Ralph.
