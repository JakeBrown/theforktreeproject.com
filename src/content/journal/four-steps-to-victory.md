It’s amazing to think that just a couple of months ago this area was a muddy mess of rubble and other building debris. Many thanks to the various volunteers who have given generously of their time to help clear this section and get it ready for planting (special mention to Tommo the Tireless). 

Now that it is clear enough, planting is well under way. Clear, dig, plant, hammer… Step by step we are making a difference.
