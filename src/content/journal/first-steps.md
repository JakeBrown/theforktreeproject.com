The high ground of the property is very exposed. A vegetation strip will be planted to act as a windbreak along the eastern boundary. 

Fencing has started along this section to protect the saplings from kangaroos - the saplings will be planted between these two fencelines. The tall post is an indication of the height of fencing required to inconvenience the very large, healthy roos around here…
