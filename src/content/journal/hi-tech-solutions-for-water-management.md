Stage 2 of the implementation of modern technology to ensure efficiency of water management at The Forktree Project: the installation of a flow meter by SA Water. This will allow us to optimise water use and plant growth in the nursery and, ultimately, the rare seed orchard, by measuring and managing how much water gets used.

Many thanks to SA Water for their continued support of The Forktree Project’s sustainability aims and, in particular, to Andy for getting the job done!
