We were delighted today to welcome volunteers from CHU’s South Australian office for their second visit to Forktree. The team assisted with both tree planting and fencing, applying themselves to both tasks with gusto and enthusiasm. Many thanks to the Adelaide team and to CHU as a whole for their unstinting support for the Forktree Project. 

We hope to be able to welcome other volunteer groups from other organisations to Forktree - there is no doubt that rewilding activities such as tree planting are uplifting, rewarding and energising team bonding activities. For more information, please [contact us](<https://www.theforktreeproject.com/join-us>).
