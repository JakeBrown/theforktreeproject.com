# Corporate Events Venue — Page Content

## Heading
Corporate events venue

## Intro
Come and have your next corporate event, off-siter, strategy session or team-building day at The Forktree Project, with our tastefully restored shearing shed offering a wonderful combination of modern amenities, rustic charm and panoramic views of the ocean.
Perfect surroundings to talk strategy or re-energise your team.

## Facilities
- Wifi throughout
- 3 wheelchair-accessible toilets/bathrooms
- Wheelchair access throughout
- Main lecture room for up to 30 people with AV facilities
- 4 break-out rooms for smaller groups
- Open plan kitchen and common room for onsite catering with tea and coffee-making facilities, microwave, fridge and oven
- Covered verandah with sea views to take time out or run session outside
- BBQ and pizza oven
- Ample on-site parking
- A range of experiential activities involving nursery work or tree planting available upon request

## Location Note
One hour from Adelaide CBD going against the flow of traffic!

## Testimonial 2 — EPA SA
The Environment Protection Authority thanks the Forktree Project team for providing a meaningful setting for our senior leadership group at Forktree. The day offered a valuable opportunity to step outside our usual environment and reconnect with the land in a way that was practical, reflective and genuinely meaningful.
Through expertly guided, hands-on nursery activities, time spent working with the soil, and shared conversations in a natural setting, leaders were encouraged to slow down, observe the impact of the season on the land, and engage with environmental stewardship at a human scale. The structure of the day supported thoughtful discussion while allowing space for connection and reflection.
The experience reinforced the importance of grounding environmental leadership in real-world systems and care for land beyond the office or boardroom. It provided a shared reference point for reflection, learning and collective purpose.
We extend our thanks to Tim, Liz, Fleur, and the Forktree team for the care, expertise and generosity they brought to the day. The team's thoughtful guidance and strong connection to place created an experience that will stay with our leaders well beyond the visit.
— The Environment Protection Authority SA
