# Support offline volunteer sign-ins

Follow up on Liz's request for Forktree staff to record volunteer arrivals on phones when there is no Wi-Fi. Provide a privacy-conscious offline-capable sign-in experience that queues name/phone attendance records locally, syncs safely when connectivity returns, clearly shows pending/synced state, and avoids duplicate records. Confirm device/browser expectations before implementation. Acceptance: installed/cached page opens without connectivity after setup, queued records survive a restart, sync is idempotent, and users can see whether each sign-in has synced.
