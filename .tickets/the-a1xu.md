# Define volunteer registration retention and deletion policy

Agree with Liz how long volunteer registration records, including emergency contact, accessibility, medical, date-of-birth, and WWCC information, should be retained and how staff can delete or anonymise expired records. The initial implementation does not delete records automatically.

## Acceptance Criteria

A documented retention period and deletion/anonymisation process are approved; the privacy statement is checked against that policy; any automation or staff workflow needed to enforce it is implemented and verified.
